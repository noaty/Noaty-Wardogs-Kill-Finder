NOATY KILL FINDER 1.2.0

1. Extract the entire ZIP. Keep _internal beside Noaty Kill Finder.exe.
2. Open the EXE, choose a video, then SCAN FOR KILLS.
3. Review detections. Remove incorrect markers or add missed ones.
4. EXPORT KILL HIGHLIGHTS creates one edited MP4 with audio.
   Defaults: 5 seconds before, 5 seconds after, link kills within 60 seconds.
   Edit those fields before export. Choose where to save in the save window.
5. SAVE VIDEO WITH MARKERS keeps the whole recording and adds chapters for OBS Highlight Finder.html.

Highlights are re-encoded; full marked copies are not. Originals remain unchanged.
First use may ask to download FFmpeg. No Python installation needed.

AUDIO DETECTION (default): Scan for kills listens for the confirmation chime.
Detection settings lets you choose Auto, All tracks, or a specific audio track.
If no chimes are found, try All tracks or select Notification text.
Muted or filtered game audio may not contain the chime. Nearby simultaneous
kills can share one marker. Review the timestamps before exporting.
Audio detection contributed by whiskysix (Whisky).

REVIEW FLAGS
Audio + visual check is the default. Audio finds candidates quickly; nearby frames check KILL CONFIRMED text. Amber candidates are unverified, not necessarily wrong. Switch ON to keep a result, or OFF to exclude it. Remove all OFF removes excluded rows. Restore results restores scan decisions. Only included markers are exported. Audio-only and visual-only are available in Detection method.
