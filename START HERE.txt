NOATY KILL FINDER 1.0.0

Extract this entire folder. Run Noaty Kill Finder.exe.
Keep _internal beside the EXE.

1. Drop a recording, then SCAN FOR KILLS.
2. Review the automatically detected markers.
3. SAVE VIDEO WITH MARKERS creates a new, full recording with chapters.
4. OPEN MARKER VIEWER, then drop that marked copy into the viewer.

No AI. No uploads. No re-encoding. Source footage stays untouched.
NEW CLIP clears the workspace for another recording.

FIRST USE: Click Yes if asked to download the scanner component.
Setup is automatic. No Python or command-line steps are needed.
The download comes from PyPI and is checksum-verified. Internet is needed
only for setup; your recordings never leave your PC.
You can also use your own ffmpeg.exe beside the app or on PATH.

Review the results: hidden or overlapping notifications can be missed.
Allow approximately the source recording's size in free disk space.
See README.md for limitations, troubleshooting and source instructions.
