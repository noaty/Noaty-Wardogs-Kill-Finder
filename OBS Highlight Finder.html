Optional: manually mark future recordings in OBS
  1. In OBS, open Settings → Output → Recording and set Recording Format to Hybrid MP4 (Hybrid MOV also works).
  2. Open Settings → Hotkeys, find Add chapter marker, and assign an unused key.
  3. Press that key after each kill or highlight while recording. Stop the recording normally so OBS saves the chapters.
  4. Open the finished recording here. In Filmora, use these times on the original, untrimmed clip placed at the start of your timeline. Add Filmora markers before cutting or rearranging.

This app reads markers already saved by OBS; it does not listen for keys itself. It cannot recover past key presses from recordings without saved chapters. Browser playback depends on the video codec; timestamp extraction does not.
